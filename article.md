# TRAVEL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

### PROBLEM STATEMENT:

These days many travel booking applications are available, but most of them provide only limited services.

For booking tickets, hotels, and local travel, users need to use different applications, which makes travel planning confusing.

There is no single platform where users can manage their complete trip easily.

Because of this, travel planning becomes time-consuming and difficult.

So, there is a need for a simple system that helps users plan and manage their entire journey in one place.

### CONVENTIONAL SOLUTION:

In the conventional method, people plan their trips using multiple applications and manual methods.

Tickets are booked using one app, hotels using another app, and local travel is managed separately.

For food and tourist places, people depend on online searches or suggestions from others.

Expenses are usually written manually or stored in notes.

This method is not well organized and creates confusion during travel.

### DRAW BACKS OF CONVENTIONAL APPROACH:

The main drawback of the conventional approach is that it requires using many different applications.

This increases confusion and wastes time.

Managing bookings and expenses manually may lead to mistakes.

Important travel details may be missed.

Overall, the conventional approach is not efficient for complete trip management.

### YOUR APPROACH/PROPOSED SOLUTION:

To overcome these problems, we developed a Travel Management System.

This system helps users manage their entire trip in one application.

Users can plan travel, select transport, book local stay, use local travel services, check food and attractions, upload photos, and track expenses.

Our approach reduces confusion and makes travel planning simple and easy.

### UNIQUE FACTOR:

The unique feature of our project is that it combines all travel-related services into a single system.

Unlike other applications that focus only on booking, our project helps in complete trip planning.

Users do not need to switch between different apps.

This makes the system user-friendly, time-saving, and easy to use.
